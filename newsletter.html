V2X Funding Opportunity: Safe Streets and Roads for All

If you are interested in deploying vehicle-to-everything (V2X) technology to improve roadway safety in your community, consider applying for a grant through the Safe Streets and Roads for All (SS4A) program.

The U.S. Department of Transportation (USDOT) recently released the fiscal year (FY) 2026 Notice of Funding Opportunity (NOFO) for the program.

The SS4A program funds regional, local, and Tribal initiatives through grants to improve safety and prevent roadway fatalities and serious injuries. Approximately $1 billion remains available for the next funding round.

 

Understanding the NOFO: What types of work does the grant fund?

The SS4A program provides funding for two main grant types:  

  • Planning and Demonstration Grants are used to develop, complete, or supplement an  Action Plan, as well as carry out demonstration activities that inform an Action Plan. 
  • Implementation Grants are used to implement strategies or projects that are consistent    with an existing Action Plan and may also bundle funding requests for supplemental planning  and demonstration activities that inform the Action Plan.

The NOFO is posted on Grants.gov and is also available for viewing on the SS4A website. 

The deadline for applications is May 26, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. (ET).

 

SS4A Projects can Further V2X Applications and Demonstrations

The SS4A program offers eligible applicants a significant opportunity to fund V2X initiatives.

Previously awarded SS4A grants for V2X-related projects include:

  • City of Bellevue, Washington, “Bellevue Safe Access for Everyone” (SS4A Implementation Grant, FY 2023)
  • Contra Costa County, California, “Contra Costa Transportation Authority Safety Action Plan Update: Enhancing Road Safety and Emergency Response with C-V2X Technology” (SS4A Planning and Demonstration Grant, FY 2025)
  • University of Hawai’i, “Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) Application Demonstration to Enhance Safety Performance for All Road Users” (SS4A Planning and Demonstration Grant, FY 2025)
  • City of Chattanooga, Tennessee, “Roadway Safety Design Technology Platform” (SS4A Planning and Demonstration Grant, FY 2025)
  • University of Utah, “Integrated AI Computing and V2X Infrastructure Systems for Safer Streets” (SS4A Planning and Demonstration Grant, FY 2025)
