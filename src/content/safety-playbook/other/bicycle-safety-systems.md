Bicycle safety systems are designed to enhance the protection and visibility of cyclists on the road through a combination of advanced technologies and smart accessories. Radar systems alert cyclists to approach vehicles, improving situational awareness and helping prevent collisions. Smart helmets and crash sensors provide built-in lights, turn signals, and emergency alerts to notify others in case of crashes. Devices like Strava Beacon share a rider’s real-time location with selected contacts, ensuring that someone is aware of their whereabouts during a ride.

Additional safety features include lighted wheels and clothing with integrated LEDs, which make cyclists highly visible from all angles, and reflective gear that increases visibility in low-light conditions. Innovative airbag systems offer head and neck protection by inflating during a crash, reducing the severity of injuries. Together, these bicycle safety systems promote safer riding, improve awareness of surrounding traffic, and reduce the risk of crashes for cyclists.

This technology implements bike lanes, bicycle detection at intersections, and automated warning systems to enhance the safety of cyclists to reduce the risk of collisions between vehicles and cyclists, improve visibility and awareness for both cyclists and drivers, and promote safer cycling environments.
