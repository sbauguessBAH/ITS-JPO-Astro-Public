The eSTORM system is designed to enhance traffic management and operational efficiency before, during, and after severe storms and hurricane events. It provides transportation agencies and emergency managers with the tools needed to quickly assess damage, prioritize response actions, and restore normal traffic operations as safely and efficiently as possible.

A primary function of eSTORM is the rapid restoration of traffic signals damaged by extreme weather. Through its mobile application, field personnel can immediately record signal status, damage levels, and repair needs. This real time data entry allows maintenance crews and traffic engineers to quickly identify critical locations and dispatch resources where they are most needed. eSTORM also supports the removal of lane blocking debris that can disrupt traffic flow and hinder emergency response. By documenting debris locations and roadway conditions, the system helps agencies coordinate cleanup efforts and reopen lanes more quickly. For longer term roadway closures, eSTORM plays an important role in detour management. The system helps agencies plan, implement, and monitor detours, ensuring continuity of travel for the public while supporting emergency operations and economic recovery in affected areas.

Enhanced data collection is a key strength of the eSTORM system. The combination of a mobile application, a statewide traffic signal database, and real time dashboards provide a comprehensive view of system conditions. Emergency managers and transportation officials can monitor restoration progress, assess network wide impacts, and make informed decisions based on up-to-date information.
