Road Weather Management is a critical domain within intelligent transportation systems (ITS), dedicated to mitigating the effects of adverse weather on road safety and mobility. Weather conditions such as snow, ice, heavy rain, fog, and high winds can significantly impair visibility, traction, and traffic flow, leading to increased risks of accidents and disruptions. Road Weather Management leverages a combination of cutting-edge sensors, data analytics, and communication tools to monitor, predict, and respond to these challenges in real time, ensuring that transportation systems operate smoothly under varying weather conditions.

At the heart of Road Weather Management are tools such as Road Weather Information Systems (RWIS) that collect data on pavement temperatures, surface conditions, and atmospheric variables from roadside sensors. These systems integrate with AI-based predictive models to forecast weather impacts on specific road segments, enabling transportation agencies to anticipate and mitigate issues proactively. For instance, RWIS data can guide winter road maintenance by optimizing the timing and amount of salt or de-icing chemicals applied to roads, as well as strategically deploying snowplows to high-priority areas. Non-invasive sensors and precipitation monitor further enhance situational awareness by providing constant updates on changing conditions, helping decision-makers adjust responses dynamically.

By integrating weather forecasting with real-time operational insights, Road Weather Management reduces roadway risks, minimizes delays, and enables cost-effective resource allocation for road agencies. Its ability to extend the life of infrastructure through proactive maintenance and prevent weather-related accidents highlights its value to modern transportation systems. As climate and weather patterns increasingly challenge transportation networks, Road Weather Management provides a highly adaptive and resilient framework, ensuring the safety and reliability of roads for all users year-round.
