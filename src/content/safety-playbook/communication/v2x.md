Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) is an advanced communication technology that enables vehicles to interact with various elements of the transportation ecosystem, including other vehicles, roadway infrastructure, pedestrians, and even remote networked systems. By exchanging real-time data such as location, speed, traffic signal status, and environmental conditions, V2X systems create an intelligent network where road users and infrastructure collaborate to enhance safety and efficiency.

V2V, or Vehicle-to-Vehicle communication, focuses on direct data exchange between vehicles to prevent collisions and ensure efficient traffic flow. V2I, or Vehicle-to-Infrastructure communication, connects vehicles to traffic lights, roadside units, and other infrastructure for optimized signal timing, road hazard warnings, and adaptive traffic management. Similarly, V2P, or Vehicle-to-Pedestrian communication, protects vulnerable road users by enabling vehicles to identify and interact with pedestrians or cyclists through wearable devices or mobile apps. Finally, V2N, or Vehicle-to-Network communication, provides cloud-based services such as weather updates, navigation assistance, and congestion alerts. This seamless and interconnected communication system is foundational to implementing smart cities and advancing autonomous vehicle technology, ensuring safer roads and more efficient travel for everyone.
