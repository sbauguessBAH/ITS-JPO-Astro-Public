Traffic management cameras play a critical role in modern transportation systems by providing continuous monitoring and data collection for roadways and intersections. These cameras analyze vehicle movements to determine traffic flow patterns, peak hours, average speeds, and congestion levels, allowing transportation agencies to optimize signal timing and manage traffic efficiently.

In addition to monitoring traffic, these cameras detect incidents such as crashes, stalled vehicles, or other hazards, enabling rapid responses from authorities to reduce delays and enhance safety. Some cameras are also used for speed enforcement, monitoring vehicle speeds and issuing citations to help maintain safe driving behaviors. Traffic management cameras further support intersection management by analyzing near-real-time movements of vehicles and pedestrians, informing adjustments to traffic signals for smoother and safer flow. They collect valuable data on vehicle types, speeds, and license plates, which are used for planning, policymaking, and evaluating traffic control measures. By combining surveillance, data collection, and enforcement functions, traffic management cameras are integral to enhancing roadway safety, efficiency, and overall traffic operations.
