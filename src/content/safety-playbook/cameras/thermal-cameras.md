Thermal cameras are powerful tools in transportation systems, providing enhanced monitoring and safety capabilities by detecting heat signatures from vehicles, infrastructure, and pedestrians. In public transportation, these cameras help identify incidents such as overheating components or stopped vehicles on tracks, allowing operators to respond quickly and prevent crashes.

Railway systems benefit from thermal imaging by detecting people or obstacles on tracks, reducing the risk of collisions and improving overall safety. On roadways, thermal cameras enhance monitoring for roadside maintenance, identifying heat patterns that may indicate potential mechanical or infrastructure issues. They are particularly valuable for pedestrian protection, allowing drivers to see pedestrians and other objects in low-light or night-time conditions, which improves reaction time and reduces crashes. In addition to safety applications, thermal cameras support infrastructure monitoring by assessing road conditions and detecting hazards, contributing to more efficient maintenance and reduced crash rates. By providing real-time visualizations of thermal patterns, these cameras enable transportation agencies to make data-driven decisions, enhance operational efficiency, and maintain safer travel environments for both vehicles and pedestrians.
