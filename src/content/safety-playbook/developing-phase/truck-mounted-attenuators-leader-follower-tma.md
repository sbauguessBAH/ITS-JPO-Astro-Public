The Leader-Follower Truck-Mounted Attenuator (TMA) system is a critical safety technology designed to protect work zone personnel, emergency responders, and equipment while improving operational efficiency. By employing automated leader-follower vehicle systems, the TMA separates workers from potential hazards during activities such as lane striping, sweeping, bridge flushing, and pothole repair. For emergency response, TMAs safeguard personnel and equipment at roadway incidents by absorbing the impact of colliding with vehicles, reducing injury severity, and enhancing survivability.

The system also supports automated maintenance operations through the use of Cooperative Driving Automation technology, which includes a lead truck, a follow truck, and a truck-mounted attenuator that acts as a buffer to minimize property damage in the event of rear-end collisions. TMAs function as mobile barriers to shield work crews from errant vehicles in both mobile and short-duration operations, with automation increasingly removing the need for human drivers entirely. By integrating these technologies, the ATMA system improves worker safety, increases operational efficiency, and is widely deployed across the United States to support daily and nightly mobile maintenance operations. These applications demonstrate the versatility and critical importance of TMAs in modern transportation infrastructure maintenance.

This system involves deploying attenuator equipped trucks that act as protective barriers. It enhances safety by protecting both workers and motorists in construction zones and reducing the risk of direct collisions with work crews.
