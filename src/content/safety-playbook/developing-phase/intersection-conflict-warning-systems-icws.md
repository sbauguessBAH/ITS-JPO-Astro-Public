Intersection Conflict Warning Systems (ICWS) are advanced traffic safety technologies designed to proactively enhance intersection safety. These systems continuously monitor traffic flow and vehicle movements to identify developing hazards before they escalate, giving drivers timely alerts to adjust their behavior and avoid potential collisions. Unlike traditional traffic signals, ICWS act as dynamic safety interventions, providing real-time feedback based on immediate conditions rather than fixed control patterns.

ICWS have proven effective at reducing crashes, particularly at rural two-way stop-controlled intersections, with documented crash reductions ranging from 3.5 to more than 19 percent. By using radar-triggered beacons, LED-enhanced signs, and other alert mechanisms, ICWS significantly improves driver awareness, even in adverse weather or low-visibility conditions. They help prevent severe injury crashes that commonly result from driver errors such as running red lights or failing to stop. Classified as Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) and active safety countermeasures, ICWS are a cost-effective, federally compliant solution for enhancing safety at high-conflict intersections while supporting proactive traffic management strategies.

This technology uses sensors and signal systems to detect and warn drivers about potential conflicts at intersections, such as opposing vehicles in their path. It provides real-time alerts through signals or signs, increasing driver awareness.
