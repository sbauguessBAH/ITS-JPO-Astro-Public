Pedestrian Hybrid Beacons (PHBs) are innovative traffic control devices designed to enhance pedestrian safety and manage vehicle flow at mid-block crosswalks without requiring full traffic signals. PHBs are particularly useful in locations where natural gaps in traffic are insufficient for pedestrians to cross safely, such as mid-block crossings, uncontrolled intersections, or areas with high traffic volumes and speed limits exceeding 35 miles per hour. By providing a clear visual signal to drivers, PHBs instruct motorists to stop and allow pedestrians to cross, resulting in compliance rates exceeding 90% for complete stops.

In addition to improving safety, PHBs significantly reduce pedestrian delays by up to 50% compared to traditional signalized crossings, making them especially valuable near schools, transit stops, and other high-foot-traffic locations. The beacon system ensures that pedestrians can cross safely while maintaining efficient traffic flow for vehicles, offering a cost-effective and highly effective solution for improving pedestrian mobility and reducing crashes at critical crossing points. This technology includes traffic control devices that are activated by pedestrians to stop vehicular traffic and allow safe crossings. It enhances safety by providing clear indication to drivers to stop crossing pedestrians, significantly improving pedestrian visibility and reducing collision.
