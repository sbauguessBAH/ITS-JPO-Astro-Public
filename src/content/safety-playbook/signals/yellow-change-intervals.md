Yellow Change Interval (YCI) systems are designed to optimize the timing of the yellow phase at traffic signals, reducing the risk of red-light running and intersection-related collisions. By incorporating real-time traffic data, vehicle detection sensors, and AI-driven algorithms, YCI ensures that the duration of the yellow light adapts dynamically to the actual driving conditions.

For instance, in areas where vehicles approach intersections at high speeds or during inclement weather, YCI systems can slightly extend the yellow phase to allow vehicles more time to safely stop or clear the intersection. Additionally, YCI systems integrate V2I (Vehicle-to-Infrastructure) communications to warn connected vehicles of impending signal changes, improving safety awareness for both human and machine-operated vehicles.
