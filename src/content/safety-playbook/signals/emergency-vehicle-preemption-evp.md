Emergency Vehicle Preemption (EVP) is a critical traffic management technology designed to improve the safety and efficiency of emergency response operations. EVP allows emergency vehicles, such as fire trucks, ambulances, and police vehicles, to request priority at signalized intersections. By granting a green light to approach emergency vehicles and holding crossing traffic at red lights, EVP reduces response times and helps ensure that emergency personnel can reach incidents promptly.

The system also minimizes the risk of collisions at intersections, protecting both responders and the public. Studies have shown that EVP can lower crash rates for emergency vehicles, creating safer conditions for everyone on the road. Beyond enhancing safety, EVP improves overall traffic flow by quickly restoring normal signal patterns after an emergency vehicle has passed, minimizing disruption to other drivers. Additionally, EVP is cost-effective, as it optimizes traffic management and reduces delays for emergency vehicles, potentially lowering operational costs for public agencies. By combining real-time vehicle detection with adaptive signal control, EVP is a powerful tool that enhances public safety while supporting efficient and reliable emergency response.

This technology allows emergency vehicles to communicate with traffic signals, triggering green lights as they approach intersections and letting them pass through quickly and safely. It enhances safety by reducing response times for emergency services, minimizing the risk of collisions between emergency vehicles and other road users.
