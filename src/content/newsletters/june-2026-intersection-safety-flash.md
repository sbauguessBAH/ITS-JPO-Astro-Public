## Intersection Safety Systems Prototyping Now Accepting Proposals

The Intersection Safety Systems (ISS) Prototyping Broad Agency Announcement (BAA) is now live. Up to $17M, across all awardees and phases, is available to support ISS prototype development, testing, and deployment under real-world conditions.

The goal is to accelerate the development of full end-to-end prototype systems and their components, including sensor fusion, AI-based decision-making, and conflict mitigation. This opportunity is open to any U.S.-based eligible entity, regardless of prior Intersection Safety Challenge participation or prizes won.
