## Freight Corridor Predictive Intelligence (FCPI) Project Phase 1 Solicitation Is Live

The FCPI project is a USDOT FY26 Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) Initiative seeking to develop an AI-enabled predictive system that fuses multimodal data to forecast freight bottlenecks and supply chain disruptions along U.S. freight corridors.

The solicitation is currently open through July 7, 2026. USDOT hosted an SBIR solicitation webinar on June 10, where the FCPI project and other FY26 initiatives were discussed. For more information, visit the link below.

#### [FCPI Phase I Solicitation Info](https://www.volpe.dot.gov/work-us/small-business-innovation-research/fy26-solicitation-now-open/)
