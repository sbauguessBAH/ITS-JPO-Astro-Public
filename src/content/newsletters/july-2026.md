## ITS JPO at 2026 ITS America Conference & Expo

![Seval Oz and Sean McMaster at ITS America](https://content.govdelivery.com/attachments/fancy_images/USDOTFHWA/2026/06/13922004/6972897/seval2_crop.jfif)

*Source: ITS America Events*

The Intelligent Transportation Systems Joint Program Office (ITS JPO) joined thousands of industry professionals in Detroit, Mich., for the 2026 ITS America Conference & Expo from June 9-12.

Nearly 250 people visited the ITS JPO booth in the exhibit hall, where USDOT Program Managers discussed innovative ITS topics, such as Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X), Artificial Intelligence (AI), and automated vehicles.

In a plenary session, USDOT Assistant Secretary for Research and Technology Seval Oz and Federal Highway Administration (FHWA) Administrator Sean McMaster were among the speakers addressing the last 35 years of ITS transformation and what is coming next. In their remarks, they announced **USDOT plans to invest $20 million in the modernization of digital infrastructure across the nation's corridors.**

Conference attendees engaged with ITS JPO staff both at the ITS JPO booth and in technical sessions throughout the conference. The ITS JPO staff included:

- ITS JPO Director Brian Cronin
- V2X and Cybersecurity Program Manager Justin Anderson
- ITS4US Program Manager Elina Zlotchenko
- Knowledge and Technology Transfer Program Manager J.D. Schneeberger
- AI Program Manager Lisa Randall
- Automated Vehicle Program Manager Hyungjun Park

The ITS JPO is proud to be on the cutting edge of ITS and looks forward to continuing the exchange of ideas with the transportation professionals and practitioners who work to improve safety and mobility every day.

### Operational Deployment of an Interoperable and Integrated V2X Ecosystem

![Outdoor Demo at ITS America 2026](https://content.govdelivery.com/attachments/fancy_images/USDOTFHWA/2026/06/13922006/6972898/outdoor-demo-itsa_crop.jpg)

*Source: FHWA*

The USDOT FHWA Saxton Transportation Operations Laboratory (STOL) showcased a fully operational V2X environment deployed by the Michigan Department of Transportation (MDOT) along the M-1 Corridor and I-94. Demonstrated at the [ITS America Conference & Expo](https://urldefense.us/v3/__https://links-2.govdelivery.com/CL0/https:*2F*2Fwww.itsamericaevents.com*2Fexpo*2Fen-us.html/1/0101019f37926a17-134d2286-03c0-4b7e-ba2f-e6acafed2075-000000/chvPkVLN7toWw2_YJk9z4CTKohBeQlcbREnEDRCqP4U=452__;JSUlJQ!!DJ_L93zn18-Ojg!mAIALdMfyE_P1rp1mXIiFy0iNRMhGxytLl31vuD_WCxjec4IAKzfKGRf1pP-WzXAQn1rCAkDcqWIXOXPCcctJt8pK2j2ZA$) as part of the Interoperable Connectivity/Spectrum program, the deployment highlighted an interoperable transportation ecosystem where existing and emerging technologies from a diverse group of industry partners work seamlessly together, regardless of platform, communication method, or service provider.

STOL led the architecture design and implementation of MDOT's integrated Direct and Network V2X framework, bringing multiple connected technologies into a unified operational environment. The demonstration showed how real-time connectivity and information sharing can enhance safety, mobility, and situational awareness for road users, vehicles, and transportation infrastructure.

### Successful 2026 OmniAir Maryland Plugfest

![2026 OmniAir PlugFest](https://content.govdelivery.com/attachments/fancy_images/USDOTFHWA/2026/06/13922007/6972925/omniair-maryland-plugfest-1920-1080-var-3_crop.jpg)

*Source: Plugfest*

The ITS JPO had a strong presence at the 2026 OmniAir Maryland Plugfest where Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) was presented and vendor devices were tested. The vision and Proof-of-Concept for transforming ITS standards into an MBSE ecosystem was presented to Plugfest attendees. The presentation was well-received and led to an engaging conversation on how MBSE could benefit standards maintenance and improve standards-based V2X deployments. The ITS JPO conducted Wayties device testing in support of OmniAir device certification where vendors could test and validate their OBUs for accuracy and conformance to industry standards. This testing supports vendors in the ITS industry to give deployers more device options.
