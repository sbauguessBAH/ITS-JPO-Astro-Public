## Intersection Safety Systems (ISS) Prototyping Awards Announcement

![Intersection Safety Systems](https://content.govdelivery.com/attachments/fancy_images/USDOTFHWA/2026/10/14620307/7213986/intersection-safety-systems-logo-fullcolor-3x_crop.png)

The U.S. Department of Transportation (USDOT) is pleased to announce the teams selected for the ISS Prototyping Broad Agency Announcement (BAA). Building on the success of the USDOT Intersection Safety Challenge, the ISS Prototyping initiative aims to advance innovative ISS that can identify, predict, and mitigate unsafe conditions involving vehicles and road users at intersections in real-time and under real-world conditions. Full end-to-end prototype systems are expected to leverage emerging technologies, including sensor fusion, super intelligence-based decision-making, and conflict mitigation to enhance intersection safety.

The ISS BAA received 23 innovative proposals. Recipients were selected through a collaborative process led by USDOT, bringing together modal experts from the Office of the Assistant Secretary for Research and Technology, Intelligent Transportation Systems Joint Program Office (ITS JPO), Federal Highway Administration, National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Federal Transit Administration, and Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration.

The three teams selected are:

- Derq USA, Inc.
- University of California, Los Angeles
- University of Michigan Transportation Research Institute

USDOT awarded a total of $5.5M for Phase 1 ISS prototype development and testing in access-controlled roadway intersection testbeds. If promising progress is demonstrated in Phase 1, an additional $11.5M is expected to be available for Phase 2 prototype refinement and assessment in real-world intersections.

Congratulations to the awardees!

### Utah and Texas V2X Accelerator Sites Advance to Phase 2

![V2X Accelerator](https://content.govdelivery.com/attachments/fancy_images/USDOTFHWA/2026/10/14605465/7209155/v2x-accelerator-header-its-jpo-now-nov2026_crop.png)

*Source: USDOT*

The V2X Accelerator sites led by the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT) and Texas A&M Transportation Institute (TTI) reached a major milestone in September 2026, wrapping up **Phase 1: Finalize Design, Build and Test**. Both sites completed all required testing and demonstrated that their systems operate safely and as designed. Now in **Phase 2: Operate and Evaluate**, UDOT and TTI will focus on demonstrating intersite interoperability, assessing operational performance and deployment impacts and benefits, and documenting a reference implementation for other deployers. The third V2X Accelerator site, led by the Maricopa County Department of Transportation, is on track to enter Phase 2 in early 2027.

All Phase 1 V2X Accelerator reports will be posted [here](https://www.its.dot.gov/research-areas/V2X-Deployment/deployment/accelerator/documentation/) as they become available.

#### [Explore the V2X Accelerator Program Website](https://www.its.dot.gov/research-areas/V2X-Deployment/deployment/accelerator/)

### Connected Vehicle Core Services (CVCS) Toolset Version 2.5.0 Introduces New Features and Enhancements

![Connected Vehicle Core Services Toolset user interface](https://content.govdelivery.com/attachments/fancy_images/USDOTFHWA/2026/10/14605470/cvcs-toolset-user-interface_original.png)

*Source: USDOT*

Version 2.5.0 of the CVCS Toolset introduces new features that make it easier to create, check, and troubleshoot the standardized digital messages that connected vehicles rely on to &ldquo;talk&rdquo; to roadside equipment. The open-source, web-based toolset includes three tools: the MAP/Road Geometry Attributes (RGA) Creator Tool, which creates digital representations of intersections and roadways, along with RGA messages describing broader roadway details; the Traveler Information Messages (TIM) Creator Tool, which builds TIMs that deliver roadway advisories to vehicles; and the Message Validator Tool, which reads an encoded message and displays its contents, helping users confirm it was built correctly. New features include the ability to upload custom imagery and align it with a live map, improved drawing and editing capabilities, automatic elevation calculations, support for additional lane types, and indicators for required data fields. The Message Validator Tool also supports additional message types, provides more readable results and detailed error explanations, and improves message transmission security, helping agencies produce more accurate and reliable connected vehicle messages with less effort. For more information about the CVCS Toolset, email [CAVSupportServices@dot.gov](mailto:CAVSupportServices@dot.gov).

#### [See What's New in Version 2.5.0](https://github.com/usdot-fhwa-stol/connectedvcs-tools)
