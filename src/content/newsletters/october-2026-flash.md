## ITS4US Deployment Accelerator Now Accepting Proposals

![ITS4US Deployment Accelerator Logo](https://content.govdelivery.com/attachments/fancy_images/USDOTFHWA/2026/09/14376959/its4us-accelerator-logo_original.png)

*Source: USDOT*

The ITS4US Deployment Accelerator Broad Agency Announcement is now live! The Program will provide seed funding to up to five sites to rapidly replicate the ITS4US Heart of Iowa Regional Transit Agency&rsquo;s Health Connector project, which connects transportation to healthcare appointments. Awardees will join a cohort of deployers receiving technical assistance and support to deploy solutions within their communities within 12 months. Proposed multi-disciplinary teams must include a mobility service operator or provider, a software integrator, and a public or private healthcare partner.
