## Cooperative Driving Automation (CDA) Small-Scale Industry Prototype Solicitation is LIVE!

![Vehicle approaching a pedestrian crossing](https://content.govdelivery.com/attachments/fancy_images/USDOTFHWA/2026/08/14130569/7048776/vehicle-pedestrian-crossing-v2_crop.jpg)

*Source: FHWA*

The Cooperative Driving Automation (CDA) Small-Scale Industry Prototype Broad Agency Announcement (BAA) has posted on SAM.gov. Up to $5M (across all awardees and phases) will be available to support CDA prototype development, testing, and deployment. The goal is to accelerate the development and testing of a cooperative driving automation prototype by utilizing connectivity and automation in an automotive OEM-supplied vehicle, testing the capability in partnership with a state or local agency. This opportunity will be open to any U.S.-based eligible entity, regardless of prior participation, with proposals due by September 13, 2026. For more information, visit the link below.

#### [CDA Small-Scale Industry Prototype BAA Info](https://sam.gov/workspace/contract/opp/fa7c3868d1de4478942fab3856ce243e/view)

## National Webinar: Demystifying AI Procurement

![AI Procurement Webinar](https://content.govdelivery.com/attachments/fancy_images/USDOTFHWA/2026/08/14130576/7048777/jpo-national-ai-procurement-webinar-image_crop.jpg)

*Source: iStock/WANAN YOSSINGKUM*

As artificial intelligence becomes increasingly central to transportation, multimodal public sector agencies face unique and rapidly evolving challenges in technology acquisition. Attend this AI Procurement National Webinar on September 17, 2026, 1 to 4PM ET, to build a common understanding on issues, identify effective practices and approaches, and establish a shared language around AI procurement themes. This event is open to public sector leadership, management, and technical specialists across all types of multimodal agencies. To find additional AI procurement resources and to register, visit the link below.
